Description: 

	This dataset supports the paper "Vision-Informed Modeling of the Squeeze Flow of Soft Materials." It contains the experimental data and results used to evaluate a vision-informed 		parameter estimation framework for the squeeze flow of soft cylindrical specimens. 



	The dataset includes: 



	(1) Ground truth data used 



	(2) Estimated viscosity and slip parameter trajectories for all experimental and synthetic scenarios; (5) Short-horizon height prediction results for all scenarios.



	(3) Short-horizon height prediction results for all scenarios.
